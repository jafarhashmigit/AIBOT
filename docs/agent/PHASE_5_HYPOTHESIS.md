# Phase 5 frozen hypothesis

Written **before** independent history was fetched or scored. Do not edit this file after seeing Phase 5 results.

## Hypothesis

At eligible decision timestamps during the corrected UTC New York session, a SELL-side trade using the existing approved outcome definition has positive expected net R after the existing transaction-cost assumptions.

## Session definition (exact Phase 4)

Copied from `features_m5.SESSIONS` / `build_m5_utc_corrected_features.py`, not retuned.

1. Interpret naive M5 `time` as Europe/Athens wall clock (broker server face).
2. Convert to UTC with IANA DST rules (`ambiguous` / `nonexistent` → not a NY decision).
3. `corrected_session_new_york` = America/New_York local hour in `[8, 17)`.
4. `corrected_session_london` = Europe/London local hour in `[8, 17)`.
5. Overlap overwrites NY: `corrected_session_id == 4` means NY **and not** London–NY overlap.

Phase 5 evaluates **that exclusive New York session only**. Session bounds are not searched.

## Side

SELL only. BUY is not tested as a replacement or addition.

## Entry

Existing convention: decision at the close of bar t; entry at the open of bar t+1.

## Outcome definition (unchanged)

From `label_m5` / `backtest_rf_m5.simulate_trade` / `build_m5_side_outcomes`:

- Horizon: 12 bars (t+1 .. t+12), entry bar included
- TP = 1.5 × ATR14[t], SL = 1.0 × ATR14[t]
- Same-bar TP and SL: SL first
- Spread floor: 15 points × $0.01; extra $0.05 per side
- SELL: sell at bid − $0.05; cover at ask
- Incomplete 12-bar window or window that crosses a market gap: **ineligible** (empty outcomes). Gaps are not filled.
- `net_r` = net P&L / initial SL distance

## Evaluation (predeclared, not tuned)

- Population: eligible NY-session SELL rows in the independent file only
- Metrics: n, mean/median/total R, net P&L, PF, win rate, max DD, average trade, largest win/loss
- Chronology: three equal-**duration** blocks on `[min decision_time, max decision_time]` of eligible NY SELL:
  - early: `[start, start + span/3)`
  - middle: `[start + span/3, start + 2*span/3)`
  - late: `[start + 2*span/3, end]`
- Uncertainty: day-cluster bootstrap, 2,000 draws, seed 42
- Cost stress: baseline, 1.5× spread floor, 2.0× spread floor (Phase 4 approximation)
- Concentration: top/bottom 5 trades, top 1% of winners’ share of total R, longest losing streak

No filters, thresholds, models, or session-boundary search.
